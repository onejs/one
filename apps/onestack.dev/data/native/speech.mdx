---
title: Speech
description: Stream dictation through the platform speech recognizer
---

`One.Speech` provides `isAvailable()`, `getPermissions()`,
`requestPermissions()`, and `start(options, onEvent)`. Pass an optional BCP-47
`lang`; it defaults to the device language. The returned session has `stop()`
and `abort()` methods.

A session emits `start`, full `transcript` updates, then exactly one `end` or
`error`. `stop()` finishes listening and retains the final transcript.
`abort()` ends silently. Starting another session replaces the previous one;
its callback and session handle no longer affect the new recognizer.

On web, SpeechRecognition or webkitSpeechRecognition supplies dictation where
present. `isAvailable()` reflects constructor availability; the browser's
recognition service can still reject a session, including network failures.
Permission methods check or request microphone access. A denied recognition
service also marks permission denied. Browsers may prompt again when recognition
starts and may require a user gesture. Recognition errors preserve the existing
Speech error codes. Without a recognizer, permission reads report denied and
`start` throws `Speech.start needs an iOS or Android build`. SSR has the same
unavailable contract. Browser service availability and actual transcription
still depend on the browser, network, language, and device.
